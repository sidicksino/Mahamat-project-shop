About FocalPoint

Made for people who love making pictures.

FocalPoint is a camera and photography store based in Kigali. We stock the gear working photographers rely on, along with simple, affordable kit for anyone taking their first steps.

From a first instant camera to a full studio lighting setup, we want you to leave with the right tool for the way you work, not just the most expensive one.
